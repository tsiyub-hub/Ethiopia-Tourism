Popular Destinations

Lalibela

Lalibela is a town in the Amhara Region of Ethiopia, known for its rock-hewn churches.

Simien Mountains

The Simien Mountains are a mountain range in the Amhara Region of Ethiopia, known for their unique wildlife and landscapes.

Axum

Axum is a city in the Tigray Region of Ethiopia, known for its ancient obelisks and historical significance.
