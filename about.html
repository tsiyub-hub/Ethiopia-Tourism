Introduction to Ethiopia

Ethiopia is a country located in the Horn of Africa. It is one of the oldest countries in the world. Ethiopia is famous for its rich history, beautiful landscapes and diverse cultures.

Location of Ethiopia

Ethiopia is located in East Africa. It is bordered by Eritrea, Djibouti, Somalia, Kenya, South Sudan and Sudan. Its capital city is Addis Ababa.

History of Ethiopia

Ethiopia has a long and interesting history. It is known for ancient civilizations, historical kingdoms and remarkable cultural heritage. The country is also famous for the ancient rock-hewn churches of Lalibela.

Languages and Culture

Ethiopia is home to many ethnic groups and languages. Amharic is the federal working language. Ethiopian culture includes traditional food, music, dancing and traditional clothing.

Famous Things About Ethiopia

  • Ethiopian Coffee
  • Lucy Fossil
  • Rock-Hewn Churches of Lalibela
  • Ethiopian Traditional Food
  • Timket and Meskel Festivals
